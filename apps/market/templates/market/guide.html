Using AA MarketResearch

A task-based guide to screening, chart review, alerts, journaling and data verification.

1. Begin each session

Start Django, then open the Dashboard. Use Data Health only when the readiness banner reports a problem.

Open Dashboard

2. Find candidates

Choose the market and timeframe, apply technical filters, and open promising symbols from the results.

Open Screener

3. Review charts

Compare daily, weekly and monthly structure. Use horizontal support/resistance, trend rays and parallel channels. Saved drawings appear across timeframes.

4. Create alerts

Create a crossing alert, select Telegram/Desktop/Sound, then keep run_live_alerts --live running on the laptop.

Manage Alerts

5. Record the trade

Capture entry, stop, quantity, thesis and setup context. The setup snapshot is immutable for honest review.

Record Trade

6. Verify data

Check coverage, technical dates, provider failures and worker heartbeat. Do not screen on stale or incomplete sessions.

Inspect Data Health
